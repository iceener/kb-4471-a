Why they were merged

The triage rule merges on the subject line (decision 0001). Every subject line that night came in from more than one product: “Off by 1h” and “URGENT times wrong” read the same on a tram board and on a care-home rota. Intake also guessed the product from the subject, so some tram and rota reports were tagged sched-prod (the “tagged elsewhere” column). The place comes from the reporter’s tenant, not from that tag.

The fingerprint in #lib/kb/fingerprint already keeps them apart: place plus server code. Decision 0001 is accepted, so it is not edited here. It needs a new record that replaces it.
